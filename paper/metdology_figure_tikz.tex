\captionof{figure}{Overview of the proposed methodology. During training, each incremental task learns a lightweight TOSCA expert on top of the frozen ViT \texttt{[CLS]} token, together with a task-local ridge classifier. In parallel, a shared random projection and a global ridge model are built in the frozen feature space for exemplar-free task routing. At inference time, the global ridge router predicts the most likely task, after which only the corresponding task expert and its ridge head are activated. This removes the need to evaluate all stored experts for every test sample. Icons mark frozen components (\textcolor{blue!55!black}{\faSnowflake}), trainable task-specific modules (\textcolor{red!60!black}{\faFire}), and closed-form solves (\textcolor{green!40!black}{\faBolt}).}
